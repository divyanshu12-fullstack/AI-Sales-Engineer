Decide whether the caller has finished the conversation.
End when the caller explicitly asks to hang up/stop or says goodbye, OR when the assistant has asked whether there is anything else to help with and the caller responds with no, nothing else, that's all, thanks, okay, ok, okay thanks, no thank you, bas, nahi, बस, नहीं, मराठी: नाही/एवढंच/धन्यवाद, বাংলা: না/এটুকুই/ধন্যবাদ, తెలుగు: వద్దు/ఇంతే/ధన్యవాదాలు, ಕನ್ನಡ: ಬೇಡ/ಇಷ್ಟೇ/ಧನ್ಯವಾದಗಳು, or an equivalent clear closing with no new request.
Do not end just because a ticket was created or an answer was given. Allow the assistant to offer further help once and listen. Do not end on an ordinary mid-conversation okay/thanks when the caller is explaining an issue, giving a code, answering a question, waiting for a tool, or asking for more help. If the caller introduces another issue, continue.
When the caller is finished, trigger the configured final-call message once and disconnect; do not require another goodbye or silence.
