# About AsterByte Solutions

## Company profile

AsterByte Solutions is an Indian software consultancy and development firm. It helps startups and growing businesses turn operational problems into useful websites, applications, automation and AI tools.

Founded in 2019 by Divyanshu Dwivedi, an IIT graduate, AsterByte Solutions has worked with more than 50 clients and delivered more than 200 service engagements across development, consulting, integrations and ongoing improvements.

The company's ambition is to become a leading software consultancy through practical advice, dependable delivery and long-term client relationships.

## What the company does

- Websites and customer-facing web applications.
- Full-stack product development, including frontend, backend, APIs and databases.
- Business applications such as CRMs, appointment systems and internal dashboards.
- AI assistants, document search and AI features integrated into existing workflows.
- Process automation and connections between business tools.
- Cloud delivery, testing, maintenance and performance improvements.

The agency can deliver a complete product or contribute specialists to an existing team. The recommended approach depends on the customer's workflow, budget and technical constraints.

## How a project works

1. **Understand the problem.** Discuss users, current process, pain points and the outcome that matters.
2. **Define the first release.** Agree on essential features, assumptions, integrations and acceptance criteria.
3. **Prepare a proposal.** Provide scope, indicative schedule, commercial terms and responsibilities for review.
4. **Design and build.** Review designs and working demonstrations at agreed milestones.
5. **Test and accept.** Validate the agreed workflows and track defects before handover.
6. **Launch and hand over.** Supply custom project source code, setup instructions, agreed documentation and a walkthrough.
7. **Support and improve.** Fix qualifying development bugs free for the product's lifetime; quote enhancements separately when needed.

## Useful information for a new inquiry

Start with the business problem. Ask for missing details one at a time: primary users, important workflows, existing software, integrations, approximate budget, preferred launch date and who will approve the work. A caller can begin without a complete specification.

A requested date is a preference until delivery capacity and scope are reviewed. Do not collect passwords, payment-card details, OTPs or production database exports during a sales call.

## Working principles and policies

- Recommend the smallest useful solution before expanding the scope.
- Explain tradeoffs in everyday language and use AI where it has a clear purpose.
- Record scope and change requests so both parties understand cost and timing.
- Provide custom project source code after agreed payments are complete. Third-party components retain their own licenses.
- Keep customer records and private project material restricted to authorized people.
- Obtain permission before discussing a real client's identity, metrics or project details publicly.
- Do not promise specific business results, AI accuracy, regulatory certification or uninterrupted service without evidence and explicit agreement.

## Contact and consultations

Public support contact details are managed in the application configuration. Do not substitute the founder's personal contact details. Use the application's configured contact channel when one becomes available.

An introductory discovery discussion of up to 30 minutes is free as part of the introductory offer. Longer workshops or detailed audits may be paid. Consultation availability must come from a connected calendar or human confirmation; expertise alone does not imply a free slot.
