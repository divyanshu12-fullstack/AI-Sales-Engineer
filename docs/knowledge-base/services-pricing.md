# Services, indicative pricing and delivery

## Pricing basis

The following INR ranges help customers plan a focused first release. Each range assumes timely customer feedback and reusable standard components. The final proposal confirms the deliverables, acceptance criteria, dependencies and price.

| Service | Typical first-release scope | Indicative budget | Indicative build time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Landing page or small business website | Up to 5 pages, responsive design and inquiry form | INR 15,000-35,000 | 1-2 weeks |
| Business website with content management | Up to 12 pages, editable content and basic analytics integration | INR 35,000-75,000 | 2-4 weeks |
| Full-stack web MVP | Sign-in, 2-3 roles, up to 3 core workflows and an admin area | INR 80,000-2,00,000 | 4-8 weeks |
| Custom CRM or business application | Leads or operational records, permissions, tasks and standard reports | INR 70,000-1,80,000 | 4-8 weeks |
| Workflow automation | One defined workflow connecting up to 2 existing tools | INR 15,000-50,000 | 1-3 weeks |
| AI assistant or document-search pilot | One approved knowledge collection, one channel and basic evaluation | INR 35,000-1,00,000 | 2-5 weeks |
| Additional API integration | One documented API and one agreed workflow | INR 10,000-35,000 | 1-2 weeks |
| Dedicated full-stack developer | One developer for up to 160 working hours per month; capacity agreed in advance | INR 75,000-1,25,000/month | Availability confirmed separately |
| Technical consultation | Architecture, feasibility or workflow review | INR 1,000-2,000/hour | Scheduled separately |
| Optional maintenance | Up to 5 hours/month of monitoring, dependency review and small improvements | INR 5,000-12,000/month | Monthly plan |

Build time starts after scope approval, the agreed initial payment and required content/access are available. External approvals, customer review and integration dependencies can extend elapsed time. A combined project is scoped as a whole; do not blindly add table rows or offer the lowest price for every request. AI voice and provider charges are separate from development cost.

## What development includes

- Agreed design and implementation, responsive behavior where applicable, and testing of the agreed workflows.
- Custom frontend, backend and database migration source code relevant to the project.
- Setup instructions, configuration variable names, agreed documentation and one handover session of up to 60 minutes.
- One deployment to the agreed environment when hosting access is available.
- Revision rounds and post-launch refinements within the limits below.
- Lifetime correction of qualifying development bugs in the delivered scope.

Source code is handed over after agreed payments are complete. Client-specific custom code belongs to the client under the project terms; pre-existing reusable tools and third-party components retain their existing ownership and licenses. Credentials are transferred through an appropriate secure process, never committed into source code.

## Revisions and free updates

| Deliverable | Included revision rounds | Free post-launch refinement allowance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Websites | 2 consolidated rounds | Up to 6 development hours within 90 calendar days of handover |
| Business apps, MVPs and AI pilots | 3 consolidated rounds | Up to 12 development hours within 90 calendar days of handover |

A revision round is one consolidated list of feedback on an agreed milestone. It can cover layout, wording and behavior inside the approved scope. Unused rounds do not become a budget for new features. Free refinements cover small text, spacing, labels and comparable adjustments; the allowance is a total per project, not per request. It expires after 90 days and does not roll over.

Extra revisions or enhancements can be estimated at INR 800-1,500/hour or quoted as a fixed change request. Explain the estimate and obtain agreement before paid work starts. Bug fixes do not consume revision rounds or the refinement allowance.

## Faster delivery

An expedited schedule may be available for an additional 20-40% of the agreed development price. A delivery lead must first confirm staffing, dependencies and a revised schedule. An extra fee does not guarantee that every deadline is feasible. Testing and acceptance remain part of delivery.

## Lifetime development bug fixes

A qualifying bug is a reproducible defect in AsterByte Solutions' delivered custom code that fails the written acceptance criteria under the supported environment defined at handover. Examples include an agreed calculation giving the wrong result or a save action failing for valid input.

Correction of these development bugs is free for the lifetime of that delivered product, including after the 90-day refinement period and without an active maintenance subscription. There is no arbitrary cap on qualifying defects. The customer must provide enough information and appropriate access for diagnosis.

New features, changed requirements, customer or third-party code changes, new platform compatibility work, provider outages, expired subscriptions and infrastructure charges are outside this free-fix promise. If cause is uncertain, investigate before classifying it or requesting paid work. A regression introduced by AsterByte Solutions' own fix remains covered. Lifetime coverage promises free qualifying correction, not immediate or 24/7 staffing.

## Exclusions and payments

Domain names, hosting, paid assets, provider subscriptions, AI usage, messaging, payment-gateway charges and applicable taxes are outside the displayed estimates unless a proposal includes them. Paid maintenance adds proactive work; it is not required to access qualifying bug fixes.

The standard payment schedule is 40% at kickoff, 40% after the agreed working milestone and 20% before final handover. Smaller assignments may use a simpler schedule documented in the proposal. Cancellation is reviewed against delivered work and approved nonrecoverable costs; the agent must not invent a refund amount or approve a refund.

See [support policies](support-policies.md) for response targets and escalation. A signed project agreement takes precedence over these general estimates.
