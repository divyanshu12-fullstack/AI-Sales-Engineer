# Teams, expertise and consultation routing

AsterByte Solutions brings together specialists across five delivery teams. Anjali is the company's AI representative and routes consultations to the relevant human specialist.

## Five delivery teams

| Team | Specialists | Responsibilities and consultation topics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full-stack development and UI/UX | Meera Kapoor, frontend and UX lead; Arjun Verma, backend lead | Websites, web apps, accessible interfaces, user journeys, APIs, permissions and database design |
| Business solutions | Nikhil Rao, business systems consultant | CRMs, appointment workflows, internal tools, process mapping, reporting and requirements discovery |
| AI development | Sana Iqbal, AI engineer | Document search, RAG, assistants, model integration, evaluation and human review workflows |
| Automation and integrations | Kabir Sethi, integration engineer | Repetitive tasks, external APIs, webhooks, synchronization, retries and duplicate-action prevention |
| Cloud delivery and quality engineering | Priya Nair, cloud and QA lead; Ishaan Shah, support specialist | Deployment, CI/CD, backups, regression testing, incident triage, monitoring and performance investigation |

## Technology capabilities

- **Frontend and UX:** React, Next.js, TypeScript, HTML/CSS, responsive layouts, design prototypes and usability reviews.
- **Backend and data:** Node.js, Express, Python, FastAPI, REST APIs, PostgreSQL, Supabase, MongoDB, Redis and SQLite where suitable.
- **AI:** Retrieval, embeddings, model APIs, tool calling, prompt design, evaluation sets and reviewable AI output.
- **Integrations:** Business APIs, webhooks, scheduled jobs and provider-supported connections. Compatibility is checked per project.
- **Delivery and QA:** Git, Docker, GitHub Actions, browser testing, API tests, deployment documentation and recovery planning.

The team selects technologies according to project requirements. Confirm compatibility and scope before committing to a particular integration or platform.

## Choosing the right consultation

- A new product with unclear requirements starts with Nikhil; bring Meera and Arjun in once the main workflows are understood.
- A website or user-experience problem starts with Meera. Authentication, database or API design questions go to Arjun.
- An AI assistant or document-answering requirement goes to Sana. Confirm the knowledge sources, acceptable failure behavior and evaluation plan.
- A manual process or connection between tools goes to Kabir. Ask what triggers the workflow and what should happen if a step fails.
- Deployment, reliability and testing questions go to Priya.
- Existing-product problems start with Ishaan, who routes the issue to the owning engineer. Suspected security incidents follow the urgent escalation policy immediately.

Do not promise a particular employee, start date or consultation slot until availability is confirmed. A team routing recommendation is not a booking.

## Authority and limits

Specialists can assess feasibility and estimate effort. The delivery lead confirms schedules and final scope. Commercial exceptions, refunds and binding commitments require an authorized human decision. The AI representative can collect requirements and explain this documentation; it cannot approve exceptions or claim engineering work has been completed.
